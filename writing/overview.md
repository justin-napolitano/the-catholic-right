## What’s This Repo All About?

At its core, this repo offers curated reflections on how today’s political landscape intersects with Catholic ideals. I’m focused on the nuances of how Catholic figures and ideologies manifest in current events. I’ve pulled in various threads—from social media influence to cultural touchpoints, all to paint a clearer picture of the Catholic right as it stands now.

### Why I Built It

Here’s the deal: I’ve noticed a growing conversation around how Catholic theology interacts with modern-day politics, especially with certain high-profile figures. It felt essential to document this. I wanted to provide a resource that not only captures Bishop Barron’s teachings but also contextualizes them within the broader political framework. By doing this, I hope to foster more thoughtful discussions around these issues.
